## 100 KM RISHIKESH RAFTING EXPEDITION — Upper Bhagirathi to Rishikesh

**✅ OPERATIONAL & AVAILABLE NOW — currently accepting bookings for the upcoming October & November batches (29 Oct–1 Nov, 19–22 Nov).**

### Itinerary

**Day 1 — Long River Rafting**
- 6:00 AM: Departure from Tapovan via traveller
- 11:00 AM: Arrive at Upper Bhagirathi
- 11:30 AM: Start rafting — 35–40 km of thrilling rapids
- Evening: Reach Vyas Ghat, riverside stay by the Ganges
- Bonfire and BBQ under the stars

**Day 2 — Rafting and Exploration**
- Breakfast, continue rafting 20 km
- Reach Kaudiyala
- Short hike, waterfall exploration, visit Gartang Gali
- Riverside stay, bonfire, and BBQ

**Day 3 — Rafting to Neem Beach**
- 35–40 km thrilling rafting toward Neem Beach
- Evening: arrive at Neem Beach, stay in Tapovan
- Bonfire and celebration

**Day 4 — Departure**
- Breakfast
- 10:00 AM: Checkout & farewell

### What's Included
- ✅ Riverside stays by the river (twin sharing)
- ✅ ALL meals: breakfast, lunch, snacks, dinner
- ✅ Transportation (Volvo bus / tempo traveller)
- ✅ Complete rafting equipment: rafts, wetsuits, life jackets, helmets, paddles
- ✅ GoPro and drone footage & photos included 📸
- ✅ Expert guides, camp staff, and cooks
- ✅ Peak fees, sanctuary fees, permits
- ✅ Porter service up to 5 kg per person

### Important Notices
- Rafting location may adjust between Bhagirathi and Alaknanda River based on weather and water levels to ensure maximum safety.
- Mandatory physical fitness assessment before rafting.
- Participants must carry a valid government ID (Aadhaar/Passport).
- Any medical history or past surgery must be declared in advance.

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: What is the 36 KM Full-Day Rafting?**
A: A full day on the Ganga from Kaudiyala to Neem Beach, Rishikesh — big rapids like The Wall, Three Blind Mice, Roller Coaster and Golf Course, a hot beachside lunch, and complimentary GoPro & drone photos/videos. ₹4,999 per person, departing Saturdays (11:00 AM) and Sundays (8:00 AM) from Lenskart, Tapovan.

**Q: Where and when do we meet for the 36 KM?**
A: Lenskart, Tapovan, Rishikesh — weekends only: Saturday 11:00 AM, Sunday 8:00 AM. Please arrive ahead of your departure time.

**Q: Is lunch included in the 36 KM?**
A: Yes — a hot beachside lunch, plus dry fruits, mixed fruit juice and a protein bar during the trip.

**Q: Are there changing rooms for the 36 KM?**
A: Yes — dedicated changing tents at both Kaudiyala (start) and Neem Beach (finish).

**Q: Will we definitely raft The Wall?**
A: The Wall is navigated only when permitted and approved by the guiding team. Rapids depend on river conditions and permissions.

**Q: Do we get a certificate?**
A: Yes — a Full-Day Rafting Completion Certificate from Globesome India.

**Q: Can non-swimmers join?**
A: Absolutely yes! Full safety gear is provided (life jackets, wetsuits, helmets). All guides are internationally certified, and non-swimmers are fully briefed and looked after.

**Q: Is it safe?**
A: Safety is our #1 priority. Internationally certified guides, top-quality safety gear, and experienced river rescue personnel accompany every expedition.

**Q: Are photos and videos included?**
A: Yes! High-quality GoPro and drone footage and photos are included with the expedition.

**Q: Can I carry alcohol?**
A: Yes, you may carry personal beverages. However, alcohol is strictly forbidden during rafting hours — permitted only at the evening camp bonfire and BBQ.

**Q: Is Ladakh available right now?**
A: The Ladakh Zanskar expedition is closed for the season and will reopen next year. Our 100 KM Rishikesh expedition is live with upcoming October & November batches.

**Q: How do I book?**
